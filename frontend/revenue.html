AI Protection Formula

The revenue protection metric represents customer transactions that would have been abandoned or lost to competitors if safety stock was depleted prior to supplier delivery.

Rev_Protected = ∑ [ μ_demand × L_days × P_retail ]

Where:
• μ_demand = ML Predicted Daily Velocity
• L_days = Supplier Transit Lead Time
• P_retail = Optimal Profit Price Point
